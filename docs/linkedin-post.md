I built an AI voice agent that calls you on the phone and talks like an actual human. 📞

Meet Arya, my outbound AI voice caller.

She dials a real phone number and has a natural, back-and-forth conversation in Hinglish. In this demo she plays a Mumbai food and outings guide. What's under the hood:
→ LiveKit: real-time audio and call orchestration
→ Gemini Live: native speech-to-speech (one model both listens AND speaks, so it sounds human, not a stitched-together text-to-speech bot)
→ Twilio: places the actual outbound call

And here's the part that makes it useful. This isn't just a food bot. The same engine can be tuned for almost any business:
→ Real estate brokers: pre-screen enquiries and book site visits
→ Receptionists and front desk: answer, route, and book appointments
→ Sales teams: qualify leads and follow up
→ Banks and collections: payment reminders and confirmations

The cost? Roughly ₹3 to ₹7 per call. That's it.
